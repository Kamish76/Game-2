# 3.11: Field Interactables & The Warden (ON HOLD - PHASE 2)

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **STATUS: ON HOLD / PHASE 2 SPECIFICATION**
> As decided during planning, field interactables and manual player traversal are deferred to Phase 2 to allow rapid validation of the automated stationary tower defense core in v1.

---

## 1. The Warden Drone (Player Agency in Phase 2)
In Megabonk, player movement drives survival and interaction. In Phase 2, a mobile entity called the **Warden** provides active spatial agency:
- **Controls:** Virtual joystick (mobile) or WASD / Mouse follow (web/PC).
- **Role:** The Bastion remains the stationary anchor that must be protected. The Warden acts as the mobile emissary:
  - Navigates the arena to manually channel Shrines and crack Chests.
  - Draws enemy aggro dynamically away from vulnerable Bastion sectors.
  - Can equip active utility abilities (Blink, EMP blast, Repair beam).

---

## 2. Planned Field Interactables

### 2.1 Chests
- **Spawning:** Dropped by Elites ($25\%$ chance) and Stage Bosses ($100\%$).
- **Interaction:** Tap or stand near while holding required Gold.
- **Reward:** 1 passive Item Relic (rarity weighted by player's Luck stat).
- **Cost Scaling:** Escalates with each opened chest: $\text{Cost}(n) = 25 \times 1.45^{(n - 1)}$.

### 2.2 Charge Shrines
- **Visual:** Luminous circular altar that spawns periodically on the arena field.
- **Mechanic:** Player/Warden must remain inside the shrine radius for 8 seconds while repelling attackers.
- **Reward:** Choice of 1 of 3 permanent bonus stat augmentations.
- **Golden Variant:** Rare chance to spawn a Golden Charge Shrine granting high-tier or Legendary-level boosts.

### 2.3 Moai Statues
- **Mechanic:** Instant one-time interaction without charging delay.
- **Reward:** Immediate choice between 2–3 free Item Relics.
- **Golden Moai:** Guarantees Epic or Legendary Relics.

### 2.4 Greed Shrine
- **Mechanic:** Instant activation.
- **Effect:** Permanently grants $+5\%$ global stage difficulty and spawn aggression in exchange for an immediate cache of Gold ($+75\text{ Gold}$).

### 2.5 Boss Curse Shrine (Bloody Shrine)
- **Mechanic:** Instant activation.
- **Effect:** Immediately summons an additional copy of the Stage Boss.
- **Reward:** Defeating the duplicated boss yields an extra guaranteed Item Chest.

### 2.6 Shrine of Succ
- **Mechanic:** One-time emergency trigger.
- **Effect:** Emits a map-wide magnetic pulse that instantly vacuums every uncollected XP shard and Gold coin across the entire arena directly into the Bastion.

### 2.7 The Microwave / Alchemical Forge
- **Role:** High-order item duplication and build stacking.
- **Mechanic:**
  - Costs Gold to operate.
  - Sacrifices one random or selected item of a given rarity tier to generate a duplicate copy of another item of the same tier.
  - Limited uses (1–3 uses before destabilizing and detonating).
