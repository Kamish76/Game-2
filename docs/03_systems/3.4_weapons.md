# 3.4: Weapons Specification

## Purpose
Defines weapon mechanics, firing behaviors, slot constraints, and the initial roster of 4 weapons for the v1 prototype.

---

## 1. Core Weapon Rules
- **Slot Limit:** The Bastion can equip up to **4 weapons** simultaneously.
- **Weapon Ownership:** Each equipped weapon has an independent level ($L \ge 1$).
- **Upgrades:** When an existing weapon is offered during Level-Up, it provides randomized upgrades from its specific upgrade stat pool, multiplied by the rolled **Rarity Factor** (1.0× to 2.0×).
- **Targeting Execution:** Fired automatically without player input. Targeting calculates candidate targets within range, applying the weapon's configured `TargetingMode`.

---

## 2. Targeting Modes
| Mode | Algorithm / Criteria | Primary Use Case |
|---|---|---|
| `closestToBastion` | Euclidean distance to $(0,0)$ | Point defense against imminent hull breaches. |
| `highestMaxHp` | Scans all targets within range, selects highest base `maxHp` | Heavy anti-elite / boss execution. |
| `densest` | Spatial hash cell with highest enemy count | Area-of-effect suppression & crowd control. |
| `random` | Uniform random selection from valid candidates in range | Chaotic / secondary tracking. |

> [!NOTE]
> All weapons ignore enemies currently outside the visible screen threshold (+16px buffer), preventing wasted projectiles off-screen.

## 3. Weapon Roster (v1 Prototype)

### 3.1 Ember Staff (Starting Weapon for Kitsune Spire)
- **Archetype:** Explosive Kinetic Ballistic (analogous to Firestaff in Megabonk).
- **Behavior:** Launches an arcing fireball toward the enemy closest to the Bastion. On impact, detonates in an AoE blast damaging all entities within radius.
- **Base Stats (Level 1 Common):**
  - Base Damage: 18
  - Cooldown: 1.25s
  - Projectiles: 1
  - Projectile Speed: 140 px/s
  - Explosion Radius: 28 px
  - Pierce: 0 (explodes on first contact)
  - Range: 180 px
  - Knockback: 12 px
- **Upgrade Pool (Common 1.0× increments):**
  - Damage: +3.0
  - Explosion Radius: +4 px (+14%)
  - Cooldown: -0.08s
  - Projectiles: +1 (at Epic/Legendary roll thresholds)

### 3.2 Revolver Battery
- **Archetype:** Rapid-fire Kinetic Ballistic.
- **Behavior:** Fires rapid, successive piercing bullets in high-cadence bursts targeting the closest threats.
- **Base Stats (Level 1 Common):**
  - Base Damage: 8
  - Cooldown: 0.45s
  - Projectiles: 1
  - Projectile Speed: 220 px/s
  - Pierce: 1
  - Range: 150 px
  - Knockback: 6 px
- **Upgrade Pool (Common 1.0× increments):**
  - Damage: +2.0
  - Cooldown: -0.04s
  - Projectile Count: +1
  - Pierce: +1

### 3.3 Pulse Aura
- **Archetype:** Radial Omni-Pulsar (analogous to Resonance Aura).
- **Behavior:** Periodically radiates a 360° shockwave centered on the Bastion, hitting all enemies within its perimeter.
- **Base Stats (Level 1 Common):**
  - Base Damage: 10
  - Cooldown: 1.8s
  - Radius: 42 px
  - Knockback: 18 px
- **Upgrade Pool (Common 1.0× increments):**
  - Damage: +2.5
  - Radius: +6 px (+15%)
  - Cooldown: -0.12s
  - Knockback: +4 px

### 3.4 Rail Sniper
- **Archetype:** Anti-Material Heavy Piercing.
- **Behavior:** Fires a high-velocity beam round prioritizing the enemy with the highest maximum health. Penetrates in a line through multiple targets.
- **Base Stats (Level 1 Common):**
  - Base Damage: 40
  - Cooldown: 2.4s
  - Projectiles: 1
  - Projectile Speed: 320 px/s
  - Pierce: 3
  - Range: 220 px
  - Crit Chance Bonus: +15%
  - Knockback: 25 px
- **Upgrade Pool (Common 1.0× increments):**
  - Damage: +8.0
  - Cooldown: -0.15s
  - Pierce: +1
  - Crit Chance: +5%

## 4. Acceptance Criteria
- [x] Weapon slot limit strictly enforced (max 4).
- [x] Each weapon independently tracks its cooldown timer and executes targeting.
- [x] Upgrades only modify the local stats of the upgraded weapon.
- [x] Projectiles despawn gracefully upon reaching max pierce, range, or lifetime.
