# 3.6: Passive Items & Relics (ON HOLD - PHASE 2)

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **STATUS: ON HOLD / PHASE 2 SPECIFICATION**
> In the v1 prototype, progression relies exclusively on Weapons and Tomes. Items, chests, and shrines are documented here as architectural blueprints to be implemented once the core prototype loop is validated.

---

## 1. Overview & Mechanics
- **Uncapped Inventory:** Unlike Weapons (max 4) and Tomes (max 4), Items have no hard inventory cap. Multiple copies of an item can be acquired, stacking additively or with diminishing returns.
- **Acquisition Source:** Acquired primarily via Chests, Moai Shrines, and Post-Wave Merchants using Gold.
- **Trigger Architecture (Procs):** Items operate via an event-driven observer pattern:
  - `onHit(target, damage, isCrit)`
  - `onCrit(target, damage)`
  - `onKill(target)`
  - `onDamaged(incomingDamage, attacker)`
  - `onDeflect()`
  - `onWaveStart()` / `onInterval(seconds)`
