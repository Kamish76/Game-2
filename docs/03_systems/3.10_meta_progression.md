# 3.10: Meta Progression (STUB - PHASE 2+)

> [!NOTE]
> **STATUS: ARCHITECTURAL STUB / FUTURE ROADMAP**
> The v1 prototype focuses entirely on in-run core loop stability, endless pacing, and build diversity. Permanent meta-progression is documented here for subsequent release cycles.

---

## 1. Planned Systems

### 1.1 Silver Coins (Meta Currency)
- Awarded at the conclusion of each run based on final score, survival milestones, and bosses vanquished.
- Stored permanently in local device persistence (via `shared_preferences` or lightweight SQLite).

### 1.2 The Shrine of Heritage (Permanent Upgrades)
A tree of account-wide incremental stat buffs purchased with Silver:
- **Core Reinforcement:** $+2\%$ Base Max HP per rank (max 10 ranks).
- **Hardened Steel:** $+1$ Base Armor per rank (max 5 ranks).
- **Fortune's Call:** $+2\%$ Base Luck per rank (max 10 ranks).
- **Greed Affinity:** $+5\%$ In-Run Gold yield per rank (max 5 ranks).
- **Draft Mastery:** $+1$ Starting Reroll, Skip, and Banish charge.

### 1.3 Character & Weapon Unlocks (Quests)
- Achievements that unlock new Factor Chassis (towers) and weapons into the draft pool (e.g., *"Survive 10 minutes with only 2 weapons equipped to unlock the Rail Sniper for all towers"*).

---

## 2. Technical Blueprint
- To be implemented using a dedicated `MetaRepository` service decoupled from the in-game Flame loop.
